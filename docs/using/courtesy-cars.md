## Courtesy cars

Open **Atelier > Courtoisie** to see the garage's loan cars and which ones are free on a given day.

- Pick a day: each car is shown as _Free_, _Lent_ (with the customer and the dates) or _Unavailable_ (in service, out of order).
- **Lend** a free car: choose the customer, the from/to dates and, if you want, the starting mileage. A start in the future books the car; use **Hand over keys** when it leaves.
- **Returned**: record the car's return, with the mileage if you have it. The car is free again straight away.
- A loan that is past its end date and not returned is flagged _Not returned yet_ and keeps the car occupied.
- Two loans cannot overlap on the same car. Cancel a loan entered by mistake with **Cancel loan**.

Foremen, receptionists and above can see the fleet; lending is for the front desk and above; adding or changing cars is for workshop managers and management.

## Deferred work

On a vehicle's page, **Reporter un travail** (add a deferred work item) notes something to do next visit, for example "timing belt". Give a short title, optional details and a priority. It shows on the vehicle page and in the vehicle's alerts when the next repair order is opened. Mark it done (**Mark as taken up**) or drop it once the customer has decided.
