---
title: Export to your accountant
description: Produce the WinBIZ file for a period and hand it to your accountant, and understand what to do when the export is refused.
---

The export gives your accountant one file with the invoices, credit notes and payments of a period. Each document is exported once: it is marked as sent and does not leave again.

At the end of this page, you know how to produce the file and what to do when the export is refused.

## Export a period

1. Open Invoicing, then Accountant export.
2. Check the format. Only WinBIZ is available today.
3. Choose the period. The previous month is selected by default. You can also choose the previous quarter or your own dates.
4. Select Download, then confirm.

Result: the file is saved by your browser, and the page shows the number of invoices, credit notes and payments exported, with the first and last invoice number.

The file is not kept on our servers. Keep it after the download. While the page stays open, you can download it again.

## When there is nothing to export

The page says so when every document of the period was already exported, or when none was issued. Choose another period.

## When the export is refused

A refused export marks no document as exported. You can run it again once the cause is fixed.

- **The chart of accounts is incomplete.** Some accounts or VAT codes of your accountant are not set up yet. Ask your accountant for the list of accounts, then contact support to enter them.
- **A number is missing in the sequence.** The file never leaves with a gap in invoice or credit note numbers. Contact support.
- **A case is not supported.** A payment settled by compensation, or with a discount on an invoice that has several VAT rates, cannot be exported yet. Contact support.

Only management and accounting can export.
